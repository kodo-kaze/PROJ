# void 2: Applications of AI, Computer Vision, Physics and Econometric Methods in Crowd Safety

**Source Reference:** [ScienceDirect Special Issue: Pedestrians & Crowds](https://www.sciencedirect.com/special-issue/100PZRGHRL6)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
Pedestrian traffic is an essential component of urban mobility. With rapid global urbanization, managing pedestrian crowds and ensuring their safety and acceptable Level of Service (LoS) is increasingly critical. 

The underlying challenges of crowd dynamics are multifaceted, requiring the convergence of **Computer Vision (CV)**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Machine Learning (ML)**, **Physics-based flow mechanics**, and **Econometric discrete choice modeling**.

---

## 🛠️ Core Research Pillars & Scope

1. **Field Data Collection & Real-Time Monitoring:**
   - Automated video-based pedestrian trajectory extraction and head counting.
   - Anomaly detection, slip-and-fall recognition, and real-time crowd control intervention.
2. **Model Calibration & Empirical Validation:**
   - Using high-resolution CCTV tracking data to calibrate parameters of microscopic models (such as Helbing's Social Force Model and cellular automata).
3. **Data-Driven Feedback Loops:**
   - Real-time closed-loop systems where live sensor data dynamically updates simulation engines (Digital Twins) to forecast downstream bottleneck accumulation.
4. **Optimization Under Normal & Emergency Conditions:**
   - Reinforcement learning and heuristic algorithms for optimizing traffic signal phasing, dynamic exit gate control, and evacuation guidance.
5. **Statistical Mechanics & Econometrics:**
   - Applying kinetic gas theory, Navier-Stokes fluid approximations, and discrete choice utility functions to predict collective human behaviors during transit egress.
