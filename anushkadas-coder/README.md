## 🎯 Focus Areas & Specialization
My research focuses on **Deep Learning, Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Trajectory Forecasting, and Physical Granular Dynamics** in high-density crowds.

1. **Graph Neural Networks for Trajectory Prediction:**
   - Modeling social interactions and collision avoidance using dynamic virtual graphs and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) routers.
2. **Synthetic Data Benchmarking for Indoor Evacuation:**
   - Benchmarking time-series predictive models on synthetic datasets to address out-of-distribution shifts during emergencies.
3. **3D Granular Contact Mechanics & Physical Interactions:**
   - Analyzing extreme-density crowd dynamics ($\rho > 6\text{ persons/m}^2$) using extended Langevin equations and mechanical force transmission.
4. **Benchmark Datasets & Density Map Formulations:**
   - Density map regression on the **ShanghaiTech Dataset** using adaptive Gaussian kernels ($\sigma_i = \beta \bar{d}_i^k$).
